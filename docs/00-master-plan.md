# 00 — Master Product and Business Plan

## 1. Vision
Create India's trusted employment operating system for operational and grey-collar careers: a persistent career identity combining learning, verified work evidence, communication readiness, reliability signals, hiring, onboarding, and progression.

## 2. Users
### Candidate
Youth and working adults seeking entry, transition, or progression in operational roles, particularly in Tier 2–4 locations.

### Employer
Enterprise, mid-market, staffing, 3PL, retail, manufacturing, BFSI, and service organisations hiring at volume.

### Partner
Colleges, training centres, CSC-like networks, staffing agencies, government programmes, assessment centres, and regulated benefits providers.

### Internal operator
Curriculum, employer success, candidate support, assessment quality, trust and safety, finance, analytics, and platform administration teams.

## 3. Core Candidate Journey
```text
Acquisition → OTP/consent → profile → diagnostic → recommended role pathway
→ daily missions → work simulations → voice practice → readiness evidence
→ employer-specific SOP assessment → application/interview → offer/joining
→ first 90-day coaching → career passport and progression
```

## 4. Employer Journey
```text
Organisation onboarding → requisition template → competency and reliability thresholds
→ company SOP module → candidate matching → evidence review → interview
→ offer/joining → onboarding missions → retention and performance feedback
```

## 5. Product Pillars
### Practical work sandbox
Scenario-driven tasks representing real operational systems, decisions, exceptions, SLAs, escalation paths, and trade-offs.

### Vernacular voice AI
Structured voice interviews and coaching with separate scoring for content, clarity, structure, job knowledge, and improvement. Accent is never a quality proxy.

### Demand-led SOP pipeline
Employers define role outcomes and approved SOP content; candidates complete targeted preparation before shortlisting.

## 6. Strategic Expansions
### Workplace Reliability Graph
Measures only observable platform behaviours: scheduled-session attendance, completion punctuality, simulation SLA adherence, response consistency, and verified micro-gig reliability. It must not promise a probability of retention until statistically validated using consented outcome data.

### Phygital verification
Partner locations provide approved physical equipment and assessor-led practical tests. Digital evidence and assessor evidence remain distinct.

### Earn-while-you-learn
Paid, clearly scoped micro-internships or micro-gigs with employer-defined deliverables, worker protections, grievance handling, and transparent payment terms.

### ATS/HRMS integration
Candidates and evidence are pushed into employer workflows through consented APIs, webhooks, and mapping layers.

### Career passport and benefits
A portable history of verified skills and employment milestones. Earned-wage access, insurance, or credit are offered only through licensed partners with separate consent and no coercive linkage to job access.

## 7. Pilot Choice
Pilot Supply Chain & Logistics Operations because domain expertise, measurable SOPs, high-volume hiring, and clear simulation opportunities reduce execution risk.

## 8. Business Model
- Employer subscription for hiring workflow and evidence access.
- Per successful joining or retained hire.
- Employer-funded SOP academies and assessments.
- Partner-funded cohorts.
- Candidate premium coaching only where it does not create pay-to-access-job discrimination.
- Future regulated-partner revenue share for optional benefits.

## 9. Twelve-Month Plan
### Months 1–3
Diagnostic, one role pathway, three simulations, voice interview, admin authoring basics, pilot employer discovery.

### Months 4–6
Three to five employer pilots, company SOP modules, employer portal, interview workflow, outcome tracking.

### Months 7–9
Expanded logistics roles, partner centre pilot, verified physical assessment, integration framework, career passport v1.

### Months 10–12
90-day retention product, first ATS integration, regional-language experiment, benefits partner discovery. (The micro-gig pilot shipped ahead of this roadmap's original schedule — see "Shift Marketplace" / Phase OD-1 in `docs/generated/current-state.md`.)

## 10. Success Metrics
- Diagnostic completion rate
- Day 1/7/30 learner retention
- Simulation completion and repeat rate
- Voice assessment improvement
- Employer shortlist-to-interview conversion
- Interview-to-offer conversion
- Offer-to-joining conversion
- 30/60/90-day retention
- Score appeal and overturn rates
- Model performance parity across language, gender, geography, and device segments

## 11. Explicit Non-Goals for Year One
- Broad course marketplace
- Unverified resume database (a candidate-controlled Professional Persona
  card -- built only from data the candidate supplied via onboarding or
  resume parsing, shared only when the candidate chooses, never
  browsable or searchable by employers -- is in scope; a public or
  employer-searchable candidate directory is not. Same sharing-boundary
  philosophy as Career Passport: evidence and self-reported data the
  candidate controls, not a database anyone can query.)
- Automated rejection solely from AI score
- Lending from the platform balance sheet
- Nationwide physical-centre ownership
- More than one primary sector before evidence of product-market fit
